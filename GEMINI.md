## Project-Specific Design Direction
For this portfolio project, adopt an experimental editorial design direction combined with sophisticated interactive bento architecture.

The portfolio must feel like a custom digital product and an interactive archive of real engineering work, not a conventional developer resume site disguised with gradients and animations.

### Core Directives
1. **Asymmetric Compositions & Memorable Typography**:
   - High-contrast editorial type scales, crisp mono labels, and deliberate asymmetric grid structures.
   - Distinctive visual identity suitable for student software engineer Rithik SV (@leinhabersv-bit, VIT Chennai).
2. **Interactive Bento System**:
   - Modular, content-dense bento cards with responsive flex/grid layouts.
   - Purposeful micro-interactions, reactive hover states, and live telemetry indicators.
3. **Purposeful Technical Visualizations**:
   - In-situ engineering simulators (e.g. Quantum Tunneling transmission probability $T(E)$, Kinematic Drift ballistic trajectory, and DAG Progression simulator).
   - Three.js kinetic spatial background engine with interactive harmonic simulation modes and reduced-motion fallback.
4. **Intelligent Project Previews**:
   - Deep-dive verified case studies grounded in actual codebases, automated tests, and architectural tradeoffs.
5. **Zero AI Slop**:
   - No generic purple-cyan glow cards, no meaningless floating shapes, no template clichés.
   - Strict adherence to WCAG AA accessibility, sub-100ms interaction feedback, and multi-breakpoint responsive testing (1440px desktop, 768px tablet, 390px mobile).
